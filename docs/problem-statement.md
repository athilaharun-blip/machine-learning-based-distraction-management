# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

University students who use digital devices for studying are affected by digital distractions, especially when they have difficulty maintaining their attention during study activities.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Students may become distracted by digital activities such as social media, entertainment, notifications, or other non-study activities while using their devices. The system currently does not identify the student's attention state based on their context and provide suitable support for managing digital distractions.

## Why does it matter?
Digital distractions can interrupt students' focus and make it more difficult for them to maintain productive study sessions. Therefore, a system that can identify the student's attention state and provide context-aware digital distraction management support may help students manage their focus more effectively.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
University students may experience digital distractions while using digital devices for studying, which can affect their ability to maintain focus. Digital activities such as social media, entertainment, and notifications may interrupt their attention during study sessions. However, students may not have a system that can identify their current attention state based on their context and provide suitable support. Therefore, a machine learning-based attention state classification system can be developed to support students in managing digital distractions based on their context and attention state.
